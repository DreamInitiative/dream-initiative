01 Overview

Researching what exists
beyond the waking state.

We are a private research initiative focused on the interpretation and materialization of dream environments. Our work explores the space between human cognition and observable reality.

Dreams are complex, immersive experiences. Until recently, they have remained difficult to preserve, study or revisit.

Our research is dedicated to changing that. Through proprietary materialization systems, the Initiative is developing a method of translating subconscious environments into viewable recordings.

We believe the next frontier of exploration may not be somewhere we travel to, but somewhere we have already been.
